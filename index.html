Mother Earth is the natural world that gives us everything we need to live. She provides us with air, water, food, soil, plants, animals, and natural resources. She also gives us beautiful places to explore and a healthy environment to live in. It is our responsibility to protect, respect, and care for Earth so that future generations can enjoy her gifts too.

How mother nature can help us?

Lower your risk of Depression

Walking 90 mins in a natural area showed decreased activity in a aprt of the brain associated with depression!

Alleviate stress at work

Just going out in natureor even having a spot to view nature can improve your satisfaction and lower your stress!

Change your body chemistry for a better mood

Breathing fresh air can raise levels of oxygen in your brain, and can act like a mood booster! Exercising outdoors can make you feel calm and clear-headed.

Help you sleep better

Being outdoors will also expose you to natural light. This helps us wake up in the morning. Not getting sunlight can lead us to being tired. Being in sunlight can get a better night's sleep.

Give your brain a much-needed downtime

Spending time in nature can can help people be less stressful and let in more refreshing thoughts. Giving the body a chance to unwind!
